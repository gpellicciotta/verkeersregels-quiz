# Official Legal Sources

Snapshot of the Belgian traffic law that the quiz is based on, retrieved on 2026-09-18.

## Scope

- The quiz follows the current Wegcode (KB 1 December 1975) until it is replaced on 2027-06-01.
- This folder holds the consolidated Wegcode and every act that amended it since 2021.
- Official acts of government carry no copyright ([WER art. XI.172 §2](https://www.sofam.be/dbfiles/mfile/1500/1536/Wetboek_economisch_recht2018.pdf)).
- The consolidated PDF is laid out by Vias institute, which adds page footers and occasional explanatory links.

## Retrieval

- Source: [wegcode.be](https://www.wegcode.be/nl/regelgeving/1975120109~hra8v386pu), run by Vias institute.
- Amendment ledger: [wijzigingen](https://www.wegcode.be/nl/regelgeving/1975120109/wijzigingen~hra8v386pu), listing every amendment since 1975.
- The wegcode.be robots.txt allows `/nl/regelgeving/` with a 10-second crawl delay, which the download respected.
- The Belgisch Staatsblad site disallows crawlers, so it serves for manual citation only.

## Consolidated Text

- [wegcode-kb-1975-12-01-consolidated.pdf](wegcode-kb-1975-12-01-consolidated.pdf): Wegcode as consolidated on wegcode.be, last modified 2026-08-31.

## Amendments Since 2021

Each entry lists the act, its publication in the Belgisch Staatsblad (BS), scope, entry into force, and subject.
Entry dates marked "computed" follow the act's own clause: first day of the month after ten days from publication.
A dash means the date was not extracted from the act.

- [bvr-2021-01-15.pdf](amendments/bvr-2021-01-15.pdf): Flemish Government decree, BS 2021-02-04, Flanders, in force —.
  Load securing and height of loaded vehicles; drops a placement condition for three-colour traffic lights.

- [kb-2021-06-08.pdf](amendments/kb-2021-06-08.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2021-07-15, federal, in force 2021-08-01 (computed).
  Bicycle-street sign F111 applies until the next junction; end sign F113 removed.

- [bwr-2021-09-09.pdf](amendments/bwr-2021-09-09.pdf): Walloon Government decree, BS 2021-09-22, Wallonia only, in force —.
  Amends articles 45, 46.3, 61 and 64 to 69.

- [brbhg-2021-09-22.pdf](amendments/brbhg-2021-09-22.pdf): Brussels Government decree, BS 2021-10-08, Brussels only, in force —.
  Amends articles 11.2 and 71.2.

- [wet-2021-12-23.pdf](amendments/wet-2021-12-23.pdf): Law, BS 2022-01-24, federal, in force —.
  Opens special crossable lanes to school transport of persons with a disability.

- [wet-2022-01-24.pdf](amendments/wet-2022-01-24.pdf): Law, BS 2022-02-21, federal, in force —.
  Rewrites article 8.4: drivers may not use, hold or manipulate any mobile device with a screen.
  The PDF page also contains an unrelated act.

- [kb-2022-01-23.pdf](amendments/kb-2022-01-23.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2022-03-03, federal, in force 2022-03-01.
  Revises exemptions from seatbelt and child-restraint use (article 35.2.1).

- [kb-2022-03-09.pdf](amendments/kb-2022-03-09.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2022-03-29, federal, in force —.
  Technical vehicle requirements (KB 15 March 1968 and related decrees).

- [wet-2022-05-15.pdf](amendments/wet-2022-05-15.pdf): Law, BS 2022-06-15, federal, in force 2022-07-01 (computed).
  Rules for personal mobility devices such as e-steps.

- [kb-2022-07-30.pdf](amendments/kb-2022-07-30.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2022-09-15, federal, in force 2022-10-01 (computed).
  Ten changes, including the central lane, speed pedelecs on D9, signs E9j and A50, and restored F113.
  See the [wegcode.be summary](https://www.wegcode.be/nl/nieuws/nieuwe-verkeersregels-van-kracht-op-1-oktober-de-10-belangrijkste-veranderingen~ntwif2jyud).

- [kb-2022-10-09.pdf](amendments/kb-2022-10-09.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2022-11-18, federal, in force 2022-12-01.
  Visibility requirements for velomobiles.

- [kb-2023-03-12.pdf](amendments/kb-2023-03-12.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2023-03-16, federal, in force 2023-04-01 (computed).
  Changes eleven articles and removes the "bijzondere overrijdbare bedding" definition.

- [wet-2023-06-06.pdf](amendments/wet-2023-06-06.pdf): Law, BS 2023-10-20, federal, in force —.
  Tourist road trains made of a tractor and a covered wagon.

- [bwr-2023-06-15.pdf](amendments/bwr-2023-06-15.pdf): Walloon Government decree, BS 2023-10-30, Wallonia only, in force —.
  Amends article 11 (speed limits).

- [kb-2023-10-02.pdf](amendments/kb-2023-10-02.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2024-01-08, federal, in force 2024-02-01 (computed).
  Quadricycle definition, helmet exemption for small agricultural vehicles, and siren use by priority vehicles.
  Article 3 enters into force on a date set by a later decree.

- [kb-2024-01-24.pdf](amendments/kb-2024-01-24.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2024-01-26, federal, in force 2024-02-01.
  Replaces the entry-into-force clause of the decree of 2 October 2023.
  The PDF page also contains unrelated acts.

- [kb-2024-01-21.pdf](amendments/kb-2024-01-21.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2024-02-01, federal, in force —.
  Technical vehicle requirements (KB 15 March 1968) with related Wegcode changes.

- [bwr-2024-03-21.pdf](amendments/bwr-2024-03-21.pdf): Walloon Government decree, BS 2024-08-13, Wallonia only, in force —.

- [brbhg-2024-08-29.pdf](amendments/brbhg-2024-08-29.pdf): Brussels Government decree, BS 2024-10-03, Brussels only.
  Brussels Code of the public road; the act names 2026-09-01, wegcode.be now states 2027-06-01.

- [kb-2026-06-30.pdf](amendments/kb-2026-06-30.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2026-07-15, federal, in force the day after publication.
  Amends the new Code of 3 June 2024 ahead of 2027-06-01 and adjusts transitional dates in the Wegcode.

- [kb-2026-08-27.pdf](amendments/kb-2026-08-27.pdf): Royal Decree, BS 2026-08-31, federal, in force 2026-09-01.
  Helmet required on motorised personal mobility devices with a construction speed above 20 km/h (article 36).
